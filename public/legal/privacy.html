Fortissimo — Privacy

Last updated: 2026-10-09 · Tabulanis

The short version

Fortissimo collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not send anything about you anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your progress, scores, settings and timing calibration are stored on your own device (in the app's local storage) so the game remembers them. They never leave the device unless you choose to copy them from Options ("Copy my save"). Deleting the app, or "Reset progress" in Options, removes them.

What the game does not do

  • No account, sign-in, name, email or age is ever asked for.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.
  • No third-party SDKs for ads or analytics.
  • No network requests during play. The web version loads the game's own files from the server you opened it from, like any web page.

Controllers and haptics

If you connect a game controller, the game reads its buttons through the standard gamepad interface on your device; nothing about the controller is recorded. On phones, a short vibration may play when you hit a note; it can be switched off in your device's settings.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this ever changes, the date above changes with it and the new policy ships with the game.
